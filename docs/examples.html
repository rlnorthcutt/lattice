App Dashboard Shell

A sidebar nav that's hidden below md and pinned as a column from md up, a flex stat row, and a responsive card grid for content — all inside a single .grid. Combines .d-none/.md-d-block with .span/.md-span-*.

<div class="grid gap-0">
  <nav class="d-none md-d-block md-span-3">…</nav>
  <div class="span md-span-9 p-3">
    <div class="d-flex flex-wrap gap-3 mb-3">…stat cards…</div>
    <div class="grid col-1 sm-col-2 lg-col-3 gap-3">…content cards…</div>
  </div>
</div>

Article Layout (Holy Grail)

Three independent .span regions — table of contents, article body, related links — that stack full-width on mobile and split 3/6/3 at lg.

<div class="grid gap-3">
  <nav class="span lg-span-3">Table of contents</nav>
  <article class="span lg-span-6">Article body</article>
  <aside class="span lg-span-3">Related posts</aside>
</div>

Why layout-first CSS?

Separating layout utilities from typography and component styling means you can pair Lattice with whatever design system — or classless base like Ivy — you already have.

At lg and up this column claims 6 of 12 tracks, with the ToC and related links flanking it at 3 tracks each.

Narrow the browser below 1024px (lg) — all three stack full-width in DOM order.

Pricing Grid

Equal columns that stack on mobile and go 3-up at lg. Each card is itself a flex column so the call-to-action pins to the bottom no matter how much copy is above it — the grid stretches all three cards to equal height automatically, and .flex-1 on the body absorbs the leftover space. .order-first promotes the recommended plan to the front without moving it in the DOM.

<div class="grid col-1 sm-col-2 lg-col-3 gap-3">
  <article class="card d-flex flex-col">
    <header><h3>Starter</h3></header>
    <div class="flex-1">…plan copy…</div>
    <footer><a role="button">Choose Starter</a></footer>
  </article>
  <article class="card d-flex flex-col order-first" data-variant="primary">
    <!-- promoted to first, regardless of source order -->
  </article>
</div>

Sticky Filter Bar

A horizontally-scrolling filter row that sticks to the top of its own scroll container while a results grid scrolls underneath it. Built with .sticky, .d-flex, .flex-nowrap, and .overflow-x-auto — no JS.

<div class="sticky d-flex flex-nowrap gap-2 overflow-x-auto p-2">
  <button data-variant="primary">All</button>
  <button data-variant="outline">Layout</button>
  …
</div>
<div class="grid col-1 sm-col-2 gap-2">…results…</div>
